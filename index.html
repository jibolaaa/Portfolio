Projects

IoT · Final-year project · Team of 2 · Successfully defended

FireSafe: Smart Fire & Gas Detection System

A complete IoT safety platform built at Bowen University. An ESP32 node reads gas levels (MQ-2) and temperature (DHT11), drives tiered local alarms (buzzer, LED and LCD) and streams readings over WiFi to a Flask REST API. Readings are stored in Supabase, push notifications fire at warning, urgent and critical thresholds, and a React Native app shows a live dashboard and alert history.
